fix: stop reporting non-calls as unresolved, and exclude shipped bundles

Two defects in the analyzer, both found by measuring a real scan of
microsoft/playwright rather than by a test that could have missed them.

CONTROL_WORDS was filtering six perfectly ordinary callables out of the
extractor -- go, map, filter, range, print, println, require -- while
async, void and super were missing from it and were being recorded as
calls. Narrowed to words that cannot be a call, and added a separate
LANGUAGE_KEYWORDS set applied through is_not_a_call().

Unresolved call targets on playwright: 52,793 -> 42,026. Edges dropped from
286,707 to 251,547. Findings 1,596 -> 930 with critical unchanged at 2.

The findings fell because tests/assets/ holds a browserified React 15 build.
Analysing another project's shipped copy produced hundreds of findings about
a repository that does not contain it. Vendored bundles are now excluded,
except under src/ where assets/ is a source directory like any other.

515 tests pass.
